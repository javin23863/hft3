# FCM landscape for small algorithmic CME options-on-futures account (short premium, 0-45DTE ES/NQ, Rithmic)

*Generated 2026-06-12 by WS-0 research workflow (web sources; verify before contract decisions).*

## Findings

- AMP Futures — DISQUALIFIED for short premium. Official risk disclosure (live 2025-2026): 'AMP does NOT permit Net Short (Naked) Options Positions' [1]. AMP forum staff replies go further: Rithmic accounts are 'enabled only for long option positions' — BUY-SIDE ONLY, no selling, no spreading, no covered calls; vertical spreads and iron condors are rejected at the order router [2]. Options via Rithmic on all CME exchanges since Sep 2016 [4]. Rithmic R|API+ at AMP: $100/mo API fee + $25/mo Trader ID + $0.10/contract routing; R|Diamond API (low-latency) also offered [3].
- NinjaTrader/Tradovate — effectively futures-only. Options on futures are NOT supported through NinjaTrader Brokerage; NT desktop only displays options data / trades via 3rd-party brokers [5]. Tradovate marketing pages still claim 'Futures & Options on Futures all in the same platform' with zero detail on shorting/margin — needs direct verification, almost certainly no naked-short program [6]. Context: NinjaTrader became part of Kraken in 2025; Oct 2025 launched NinjaTrader Prop/Tradovate Prop; Mar 2026 launched NinjaTrader Connect B2B API [5]. Tradovate runs proprietary tech, not Rithmic.
- StoneX — short options permitted (with approval). StoneX Futures platform supports futures, options on futures, and spreads; publishes short-call and short-put strategy guides on futures.stonex.com, i.e., openly markets option selling [7]. Caveat for the Rithmic stack: per Optimus, the StoneX retail/GAIN division datafeeds are GAIN(OEC) and CQG — Rithmic NOT listed for that division [9]. StoneX completed R.J. O'Brien acquisition 2025-07-31: ~$900M equity + up to $143M assumed debt, +75,000 clients, >$13B client float, now largest US non-bank FCM [8]; RJO channel is traditionally options-seller-friendly.
- Advantage Futures — best institutional-lite fit for an algo short-options account. $10,000 stated minimum deposit; 'charges Exchange Minimum Margins, but reserves the rights... to raise those margins at our discretion' (i.e., SPAN with no standing house add-on) [11]. Dedicated Algo Traders program, server colocation at multiple data centers, 24-hour Global Execution Desk, two Chicago trading facilities; among highest-volume futures AND options-on-futures clearing firms since 2003 [11]. Rithmic is supported at Advantage (CME tech-vendor listing) — confirm R|API+ terms directly.
- Dorman Trading — options-capable clearing, but retail access is IB-gated. MIAX-owned since Oct 2022; ~40 platforms including Rithmic (Dorman + its IBs formally joined Rithmic's FCM & IB network) [13][12]. Account docs: margin on short (granted) options 'at least equal to the minimum margins established by the exchange... Dorman may establish requirements in excess' [12]. ~$3,000 typical minimum via IBs [13]. Dorman never competes with its IBs, so short-option permission and add-ons are set per-IB (e.g., EdgeClear policy not published) — must ask the specific IB.
- Marex — institutional FCM, wrong channel for this account size. Sold its spare FCM shell (Marex North America, left over from the $220M ED&F Man Capital Markets deal) to Robinhood effective 2024-01-03; renamed Robinhood Derivatives 2024-02-20 [14][15]. Client-facing US FCM is Marex Capital Markets, ~$8.5B client funds (Nov 2024), largest FCM open to all client types; access via IB/institutional desks; Rithmic connectivity unconfirmed [14].
- Ironbeam — strongest self-clearing + native API candidate; naked-short policy undocumented. Self-clearing FCM; FAQ: options trading 'Yes, of course'; exchange-supported futures AND options spreads tradable; real-time SPAN margining on platform [16][17]. Own REST API with WebSocket streaming + FIX — programmatic trading without Rithmic licensing — and Rithmic/CQG/TT also supported [18]. Optimus routes its Rithmic-feed clients to Ironbeam clearing [10]. Public docs never state whether NAKED short options are allowed or what multiplier applies — top question for the call list.
- Optimus Futures — IB, not an FCM; policies inherited from chosen clearer. Clearing lineup: ADMIS (Rithmic/CQG/TT), Phillip Capital (Rithmic/CQG/TT/CTS/QST), StoneX-GAIN (OEC/CQG only), Wedbush (Rithmic/CQG/CTS/TT, pitched at HNW/algo ultra-low-latency DMA) [9], plus Ironbeam for Rithmic-feed margin schedule [10]. Wedbush via Optimus is a sleeper candidate for Rithmic DMA + short options.
- Benchmark IB that disproves a permission floor: DeCarley Trading (options-seller specialist) — 'Sell options naked, we won't force you into credit spreads', exchange-minimum SPAN on ALL products, no volatility up-charges ('some brokerage firms implement an up-charge... a percentage above and beyond the exchange minimum'), futures+options in one account, minimum $2,000 ($1,000 micros) [19][20]. So short-premium permission exists at tiny minimums in the IB channel; the binding constraint is margin math and FCM risk-desk comfort, not a published account floor.
- $200-300k claim verdict: it is prudent-sizing guidance, not a broker eligibility threshold. Published thresholds found: IBKR portfolio margin $110k floor ($100k to keep adding risk); OptionStrategist: some firms require up to $100k equity for naked selling, most $15-20k [21-adjacent sources]. Margin math (TradeStation-listed, June 2026): ES overnight initial $27,557 long/$24,921 short (intraday $2,755), NQ $41,132, MES $2,756, MNQ $4,113 [21]. A short ATM ES option margins near the future; a 3-5 lot ES short-strangle book held at <=33% SPAN utilization with vol-spike buffer implies $150-300k — consistent with the claim for FULL-SIZE ES. Micro route (MES/MNQ options) cuts requirements ~10x, putting a $25-50k account in play.
- Micro options (MES/MNQ options on futures): listed by CME with weekly/EOM expiries [23]; micro E-mini FUTURES averaged >2.5M contracts/day in 2025 (MES futures ADV peak 1,141,164 in March), but micro OPTIONS liquidity is far thinner and per-broker support is inconsistent — AMP is buy-side only, Ironbeam platform natively lists options strikes, Tradovate/NinjaTrader unverified. Hard ADV/OI numbers for MES/MNQ options not retrievable this session (CME page timed out twice) — pull from Databento GLBX.MDP3 definition/statistics schemas instead (already in-house).
- Rithmic FCM network membership confirmed via Rithmic announcements + broker pages: AMP (Dec 2013), Straits Financial (Jul 2014), Phillip Capital (Oct 2014), Dorman + IBs, Ironbeam, Wedbush, ADMIS, Advantage; rithmic.com/fcms itself is a JS-walled page (1.6KB shell, curl-blocked) [22]. Net shortlist for Rithmic R|API+ DMA + short options: Advantage, Ironbeam, Wedbush(-via-Optimus), Dorman(-via-options-friendly-IB), Phillip Capital; AMP and NinjaTrader/Tradovate are out; StoneX retail works but on CQG/OEC rather than Rithmic.

## Open questions

- Ironbeam: are NAKED short options on futures permitted for a self-directed algo account, at what equity threshold, and is there a house multiplier over SPAN (1x/1.25x/1.5x) or percent-of-premium minimum for deep-OTM shorts? Does the REST/FIX API accept short option and UDS spread orders, and are MES/MNQ options enabled?
- Advantage Futures: does the Algo Traders program accept a low-six-figure self-directed account? Is Rithmic R|API+ offered (vs TT/CQG only), and what are short-option approval criteria, intraday risk limits (max net short vega/contracts), and auto-liquidation policy?
- Wedbush via Optimus: minimum account for Rithmic DMA + short options; house margin policy during vol spikes; CHI404/Aurora colocation cross-connect options.
- Dorman channel: which Dorman IB explicitly enables naked short options over Rithmic (EdgeClear policy unpublished); does Dorman risk desk impose a standing add-on over SPAN for short-gamma books in practice (docs only say 'may exceed exchange minimums')?
- Tradovate: is the 'Futures & Options on Futures' platform claim live for self-directed accounts in 2026, and does it permit net-short positions — or is it long-only like AMP?
- StoneX: can a small self-directed account get short-option approval on the GAIN/OEC stack, and post-RJO is there a Rithmic-connected retail path (legacy RJO channel)? What equity does their risk desk want for naked ES vs MES options?
- SPAN-2 migration: CME is moving equity index products to SPAN-2 — do candidate FCMs pass through SPAN-2 portfolio margining to retail short-option accounts or apply legacy SPAN + add-ons? (Not resolved this session.)
- MES/MNQ options real liquidity: pull ADV/OI and top-of-book spreads from Databento GLBX.MDP3 statistics schema for 0-45DTE strikes — public CME stats pages timed out; micro options are known to be far thinner than the >2.5M/day micro futures tape.
- Marex Capital Markets: is there any IB-introduced path for a <$1M algo account, and is Marex on the Rithmic FCM network at all?

## Recommended actions

- Shortlist order for calls: 1) Ironbeam (self-clearing, native REST/WebSocket/FIX API, SPAN on-platform, options spreads supported), 2) Advantage Futures ($10k min, exchange-minimum margins, algo/colo desk), 3) Wedbush via Optimus (Rithmic DMA, algo focus), 4) options-friendly Dorman IB or DeCarley/StoneX channel as fallback. Drop AMP and NinjaTrader/Tradovate from the short-premium lane now.
- Universal question checklist per FCM call: (a) Are naked short options on futures permitted? Net-short spreads (broken-wing, ratio)? (b) Minimum equity for short-option approval — written number vs risk-desk discretion; (c) Margin engine: exchange-minimum SPAN or SPAN-2 pass-through? Any standing house multiplier, percent-of-premium minimum on deep OTM, or discretionary vol-spike add-ons (get historical examples: Aug-2024, Apr-2025 vol events); (d) Auto-liquidation rules: utilization trigger %, warning workflow, manual-desk override, fees; (e) API: Rithmic R|API+ supported? Fees ($100/mo AMP-style?), order-rate limits, UDS/combo order support for options, cancel-on-disconnect; (f) MES/MNQ options enabled for trading (not just data)? Same approval as full-size? (g) 0DTE/expiration-day policy: forced flattening time for short ITM-risk options, exercise/assignment handling, pin-risk desk procedure; (h) Commissions + exchange fees per options contract and per micro-options contract (micro options fees are a large % of premium); (i) Colocation: cross-connect from CHI404/Aurora, direct session vs shared gateway.
- AMP-specific (only if keeping as futures-lane FCM): confirm in writing that buy-side-only applies account-wide (hedging long puts against short futures is allowed, short legs never) — viable as the futures-hedge leg broker while shorting premium elsewhere; reuse existing R|API+ integration.
- Ironbeam-specific: request API docs sandbox (docs.ironbeamapi.com), test short MES option order rejection behavior in sim; ask whether Optimus-introduced vs direct Ironbeam accounts differ in margin schedule (Optimus page implies Ironbeam clears their Rithmic-feed clients).
- Advantage-specific: ask for Professional Traders Group commission cap terms at projected volume, colocation cross-connect pricing to their Aurora presence, and whether they assign a dedicated risk officer for short-gamma algo books.
- Sizing decision for the lane: treat $200-300k as the full-size-ES prudent floor (3-5 strangle book at <=33% of $25-28k/contract SPAN with vol buffer), but plan slice-1 validation on MES/MNQ options at $25-50k equity (10x lower margins: MES $2,756 overnight) — accept wider spreads/thinner book as research cost; quantify micro-options liquidity from the existing Databento GLBX.MDP3 lake before committing.
- Feed answers into WS-0 Rithmic/FCM checklist memo (task #1); re-verify AMP policy and Tradovate options claim in writing before the memo is finalized — both were confirmed only via forum/marketing pages this session.
